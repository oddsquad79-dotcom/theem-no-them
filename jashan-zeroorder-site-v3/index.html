WHO IS THIS

ABOUT

I'm Jashan — a student who got pulled into computers and never really left. I don't just want to know what something does; I want to know why it works and whether I can change it myself.

Most of what I know came from experimenting: breaking a server config at 2am, debugging a plugin that refuses to load, tweaking Linux until it feels right, or writing a script that saves me from doing something boring twice.

I'm still early. Not pretending to be a finished developer — just building, shipping, and getting a little better every time.
